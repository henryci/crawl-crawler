Every morgue records what the character was wearing when the game ended. Across the 9,787 streak
wins on 0.30 or later, that is a large enough sample to ask a practical question: what does a winning character look like at the finish line, and how far does
it vary by species and background?

Everything below comes from the new **Equipment** tab on Streak Analytics. Each link opens it with
the filters already set, and every view can be narrowed to any species or background. <ViewLink href={analyticsLink({ tab: "equipment", ...since })}>All wins since 0.30</ViewLink>

I checked every figure against later cutoffs too. Restricting to 0.32 or 0.33 barely moves any
number, so nothing here depends on the choice of version; where 0.34 alone starts to look
different, I say so.

## Almost everyone carries a shield

At the end of a winning game, 73% of characters have a shield in their off-hand, 4% an orb, and
only 23% nothing. Tower shields are the most common single item (28%), ahead of kite shields (25%)
and bucklers (11%).

That holds well beyond the obvious combos. Fighters finish with a shield 90% of the time, but so do
79% of Conjurers, with another 10% holding an orb, which leaves just 11% of winning Conjurers with an
empty off-hand. Winning casters train Shields to a median of 15. <ViewLink href={analyticsLink({ tab: "equipment", backgrounds: ["Conjurer"], ...since })}>Conjurer kit</ViewLink> <ViewLink href={analyticsLink({ tab: "skills", backgrounds: ["Conjurer"], ...since })}>Conjurer skills</ViewLink>

The backgrounds that skip it are the ones whose weapon wants both hands. Hunters finish shieldless
54% of the time, Berserkers 41%, and Monks, who are often fighting unarmed, 33%. Species matters at
least as much: Formicids finish with a shield 99% of the time, Minotaurs 91%, Gnolls 88%, Deep Elves
74% plus 13% orbs, and only Spriggans fall below 70%. <ViewLink href={analyticsLink({ tab: "equipment", species: ["Formicid"], ...since })}>Formicid kit</ViewLink> <ViewLink href={analyticsLink({ tab: "equipment", backgrounds: ["Hunter"], ...since })}>Hunter kit</ViewLink>

Orbs are the one thing moving. On 0.34 alone they are in 9% of winning off-hands overall and about
a fifth of Conjurer and Deep Elf ones, and Berserkers go shieldless 65% of the time. The trend is
towards "something in the off-hand", not towards nothing.

Across all wins, gold dragon scales are the most common body armour (13%), followed by the robe
(10%). That split is really two populations.

## Weapons: the broad axe is the Minotaur's whole personality

Half of winning Minotaurs (51%) are holding a broad axe at the end; for Minotaur Berserkers it is
66%, with a tower shield in the other hand 72% of the time. Fighters across every species pick the
broad axe 32% of the time, with the demon trident and eveningstar next at 7% each. Hunters have
moved to the hand cannon (28%) ahead of the triple crossbow and longbow (15% each). Casters
finish with a staff, usually an enhancer staff for their school. <ViewLink href={analyticsLink({ tab: "equipment", species: ["Minotaur"], backgrounds: ["Berserker"], ...since })}>Minotaur Berserker kit</ViewLink>

Of the winning Fighters, Gladiators, Berserkers and Monks on 0.30+, 57% had no spell memorised at

## For extended, winners change gods

On 0.30+, 33% of 15-rune wins switched god at least once, against 10% of other wins, and the
destination is usually the same: the Shining One takes 62% of all second gods, most often
from Trog, then Okawaru. That is why TSO tops the god table for 15-rune wins even though almost nobody starts
there. <ViewLink href={analyticsLink({ tab: "breakdown", minRunes: 15, breakdown: { groupBy: ["god"], metrics: ["count", "median_turns"] }, ...since })}>Gods of 15-rune wins</ViewLink>
