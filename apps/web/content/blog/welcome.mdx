Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup is turn-based, and every finished game writes a morgue file that records
the whole run: the character, every skill level, every spell, every branch and when it was entered,
every item picked up. Most games throw that kind of detail away. Crawl has decades of it sitting on
public servers. Crawl Crawler is a proof of concept for what you can do with it.

## The data

The core dataset is win streaks. I crawled every game listed on
[CAO's streak page](https://crawl.akrasiac.org/scoring/streaks.html), fetched the morgues that were still
available, and parsed them into a database: just over 40,000 games, every one of them either a win
inside a streak of two or more, or the game that ended one. That selection is the point. Streakers
are consistent, high-level players, so their games are unusually good at showing how winning
characters are actually built. The streak-breaking losses are just as instructive, because they show
where strong players still die.

Two warnings. Parsing morgue files is genuinely hard, the format has changed across fifteen years of
versions, and the parser is far from perfect, so expect data errors, especially before 0.20. And
this is a hobby project on a small deployment: it may be slow, and it may be down.

## The five tools

**Streak Analytics** is the main event. Filter the games by species, background, god, version, rune
count, or final skill levels, then look at the result five ways: as a list of games, as a grouped
breakdown you can pivot, as trends across versions, as a skill-training heatmap, or as a spell list.
For instance, here are the skills Minotaur Berserkers trained and when. <ViewLink href={analyticsLink({ tab: "skills", species: ["Minotaur"], backgrounds: ["Berserker"] })}>Minotaur Berserker skills</ViewLink>

**Morgue / Character Viewer** parses any morgue or in-progress character dump from a supported server
and lays it out: overview, skills over time, equipment, and dungeon progression. Here is the
highest-scoring win in the streak set, a 15-rune Gnoll Air Elementalist of Cheibriados. <ViewLink href={morgueLink({ hash: "d8543b11c493462cc13f3d5c55ebba84973ac9972fe1a7451d808ece5c93fb74", tab: "skills" })}>Open that morgue</ViewLink>

**Combo Records** is the CAO combo high-score table made sortable and filterable, with removed
species and backgrounds marked. <ViewLink href={recordsLink({ species: "Mi", background: "Be" })}>The Minotaur Berserker record</ViewLink>

**Player Summary** reads a player's public scoring page and shows their wins over time, streaks and
combo coverage. <ViewLink href={playerLink("Sergey")}>The most prolific streaker</ViewLink>

**The Appeal** is three proposals for the DCSS developer community about making this kind of data
easier to work with. If you only read one page besides this one, make it [that one](/appeal).

## How to use it

Start from a question, not a page. The home page and every page's guide (the pulsing question mark
in the header) open with example questions, each linked to the view that answers it. Click one,
then change the filters until it answers your question instead.

Everything you set is in the page URL: the filters, the tab, the grouping. The **Copy link** button on each page hands that exact view to someone else, or to
future you. A finding is a link.

If something looks wrong, it may well be. Open an
[issue on GitHub](https://github.com/henryci/crawl-crawler/issues/new/choose) with the link, and the
morgue URL if there is one. The parser gets better one broken morgue at a time.

## What this blog is for

Short posts that each answer one question with the tool, every number linked to the live view that
produced it, so you can check the claim and push it further. The first one is about what winning
